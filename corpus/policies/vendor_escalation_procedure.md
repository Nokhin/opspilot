# Vendor Escalation Procedure

## 1. Supplier involvement
The service owner confirms whether a third-party dependency may contribute to the incident and records the basis. Label a suspected supplier cause as unconfirmed until verified. Open the supplier contact path using the approved supplier directory and retain its reference. Supplier response is additional evidence; HarbourLink remains accountable for internal coordination and customers. No supplier credentials, individual contacts or contract-specific service credits are defined in this corpus. External information may support investigation but never replace internal escalation obligations.

## 2. Supplier notification deadlines
For a vendor-related SEV1 disruption, the service owner must contact the supplier incident channel within 10 minutes of threshold detection. For SEV2, contact the supplier within 30 minutes. Include HarbourLink incident ID, supplier service, symptoms, start time, severity, impact estimate and sanitised diagnostic evidence. Record the supplier reference and acknowledgement time. These deadlines operate alongside POL-INC-002 internal notifications; a supplier ticket alone does not satisfy notification of Operations, Customer Service or Risk & Compliance.

## 3. Missing supplier response
If a SEV1 supplier notification has no acknowledgement after 15 minutes, use the supplier's alternate escalation channel and inform the Operations Duty Manager. For SEV2, escalate after 30 minutes without acknowledgement. Record attempted channels and outcomes. Continue recovery and customer updates even if the supplier has no estimated restoration time. The approved directory provides contact methods; the assistant must not invent a phone number. Supplier status-page observations should be timestamped and clearly distinguished from internal confirmed facts.

## 4. Sharing diagnostic evidence
Share only the minimum diagnostics necessary for investigation and remove customer identifiers, payment credentials and secrets. The service owner checks the recipient and approved transfer channel. Risk & Compliance approves exceptions involving sensitive evidence. Retain a record of what was shared, with whom and when under POL-EVD-001. Never paste unrestricted production logs into a public chat or external AI tool. A recommendation to send evidence is not an executed transfer and cannot override the evidence-handling controls.

## 5. Recovery ownership
The supplier may restore its component, but the HarbourLink recovery owner validates the full customer journey and reconciliation before resolution. Do not infer internal recovery from a supplier's status page alone. Record the supplier's recovery timestamp separately from HarbourLink's validated restoration. Follow POL-REC-001 observation windows and POL-COM-001 notices. If interruption persists, assess POL-BCP-001 alternatives. Historical vendor-related incident metrics can inform planning but do not establish a contractual service-level guarantee or approved restoration promise.

## 6. Supplier review
For SEV1 or repeated supplier disruption, include supplier timeline, acknowledgement delays and dependency safeguards in the POL-REV-001 review. The service owner records corrective actions for supplier management and separates confirmed cause from hypotheses. Store supplier references with the incident evidence, not as customer-facing links by default. This procedure does not set contract penalties, compensation amounts, or a universal entitlement to service credits. Refer missing contract terms to the authorised commercial owner instead of inventing them.
