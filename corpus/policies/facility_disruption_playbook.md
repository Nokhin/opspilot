# Facility Disruption Playbook

## 1. Safety-first triage
Facilities confirms whether equipment, power, access or environmental conditions prevent safe customer service. Credible immediate danger to people is SEV1 regardless of downtime or customer count under POL-INC-001. Staff follow the approved site emergency plan and the instructions of authorised safety personnel. This corpus does not contain site evacuation routes or equipment repair steps; an assistant must not invent them. Record affected site, service, observed hazard, discovery time and interim controls without exposing personal details.

## 2. Operational escalation
For a SEV1 facility disruption, notify the Operations Duty Manager and Facilities recovery team within 5 minutes, Customer Service Lead within 10 minutes, Risk & Compliance within 15 minutes and Executive Duty Sponsor within 30 minutes under POL-INC-002. A single-site service disruption without immediate danger is at least SEV2. Record confirmed hazards separately from suspected causes. Supplier involvement follows POL-VEN-001 but does not transfer responsibility for site safety away from HarbourLink.

## 3. Site closure and alternatives
Only the authorised site owner can approve temporary closure or service restrictions with Facilities safety advice. Operations coordinates approved alternatives and verifies the receiving site's safe capacity under POL-BCP-001. Do not redirect customers to an assumed available site or advise staff to bypass access or safety controls. The assistant can explain required approvals but cannot close a site, change equipment settings or issue emergency commands. Customer instructions must be consistent across signage, service channels and the contact centre.

## 4. Customer updates
The Customer Service Lead confirms the affected site/service, safe alternatives and next update time. SEV1 first notice is due within 15 minutes and updates every 30 minutes; SEV2 first notice within 30 minutes and updates every 60 minutes under POL-COM-001. Avoid publishing unverified repair forecasts or identifying affected individuals. If capacity is uncertain, state the uncertainty before encouraging travel to another centre. Restoration messages distinguish site reopening from completion of investigation and outstanding follow-up.

## 5. Return to service
Facilities verifies safe operation and records the checks and authorising owner before recommending reopening. Operations confirms customer-service capacity, reconciles diverted appointments and applies the observation requirements in POL-REC-001 where service availability was affected. A contractor's completion note alone does not prove safe customer operation. An assistant's historical incident average cannot approve reopening. Preserve relevant logs, inspection notes, closure decisions and customer messages under POL-EVD-001.

## 6. Learning and evidence
SEV1, safety-related and recurring disruptions are assessed for review under POL-REV-001. Include equipment dependency, detection, access controls, alternate-site capacity and the timeline of safe return. Keep evidence for 180 days after resolution unless subject to a legal hold. Historical facility metrics should disclose site/service and opened_at window so comparisons are meaningful. This playbook is fictional and cannot establish a real site's legal safety duties, evacuation route, compensation schedule or vendor-specific repair instruction.
