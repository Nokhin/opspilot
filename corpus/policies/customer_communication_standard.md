# Customer Communication Standard

## 1. Communication ownership
The Customer Service Lead owns customer-facing incident messages and coordinates with the incident coordinator. The recovery owner confirms technical facts; Risk & Compliance reviews sensitive integrity statements. Use approved service channels and the status page rather than an analyst's personal account. The message log records author, approval, audience, channel and publication time. An assistant may propose wording, but a human publishes and approves it. Internal escalation under POL-INC-002 and public customer updates are distinct activities with distinct clocks.

## 2. First notice and update cadence
For SEV1 customer-impact incidents, issue the first customer notice within 15 minutes of threshold detection and subsequent updates at least every 30 minutes while impact continues. For SEV2, issue the first notice within 30 minutes and update at least every 60 minutes. If no new facts are available, say investigation continues and give the next update time. A restoration notice does not replace a missing earlier update. Record any missed deadline and its reason in the timeline for review.

## 3. Message content
Each notice states the affected service or site, the customer-visible symptom, the known start time if verified, available safe alternatives and the next update time. Label impact counts as estimates where appropriate. Do not promise a restoration time without the recovery owner's confirmed basis. A historical median or p90 is not an approved restoration estimate. Explain uncertainty plainly and avoid blaming a supplier before the cause is confirmed. Do not include customer names, identifiers, payment details, log excerpts or credentials.

## 4. Restoration message
The Customer Service Lead publishes restoration only after the recovery owner confirms stable service under POL-REC-001. State which service is restored and whether reconciliation, follow-up or investigation continues. Provide a route for customers whose transactions remain unresolved. The message must not imply that all financial or data-integrity issues are resolved merely because the site is available. The coordinator records the restoration message as evidence. Any later recurrence receives a fresh update and is linked to the incident timeline.

## 5. Audience and accessibility
Use plain language that customers can understand and an accessible text format on the status page. For a physical-site interruption, coordinate signage and contact-centre guidance so customers receive consistent information. Tell staff the safe service alternative before opening it to customers. Where impact scope is uncertain, identify the affected service without inventing a count. Route individual customer claims to Customer Service for review; this standard establishes communication content and timing, not compensation amounts or a universal payment promise.

## 6. Evidence and review
Retain approved message versions, approval records and publication times under POL-EVD-001. Include first-notice time, cadence gaps and restoration wording in the post-incident review required by POL-REV-001. Compare observed response with the current communication standard without assuming older events were governed by this version. The incident coordinator confirms handover ownership for the next notice during shift changes. Messages drawn from external news must not be presented as HarbourLink internal facts without internal verification.
