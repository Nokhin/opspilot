# Fictional enterprise: HarbourLink Services

HarbourLink Services is an invented Hong Kong multi-site service enterprise with
four customer centres, an online booking/payment service and a contact centre.
It provides appointment-based community services and renewals. It is not affiliated
with a real organisation; all procedures, numbers, vendors and incidents are synthetic.

Functions: Operations, Customer Service, Digital Services, Facilities, Risk & Compliance.
Sites: Island Centre, Kowloon Centre, Tuen Mun Centre, Sha Tin Centre, Online, Shared Services.
Services: Booking Portal, Payment Gateway, Queue System, Contact Centre, Site Equipment,
Customer Notifications, Reconciliation Feed, Vendor Connectivity.

Severity is based on impact, duration and safety, not ownership. Policies effective
2026-07-01 form a current-procedure snapshot. The incident snapshot runs from
2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30; default relative windows use 2026-10-01T00:00:00Z and
are explicitly reported. Applying current policy to old incidents is analytical,
not proof of past compliance. Customer impact counts are incident exposures and
cannot be summed as unique people. Downtime is service unavailability, not necessarily
the full opened-to-resolved duration. Open incidents and some resolved incidents
have missing downtime; metric denominators disclose this.

The corpus intentionally does not establish compensation amounts, passwords,
insurance entitlements, statutory reporting deadlines, or employee benefits.
